# 🚗 Understanding EV Adoption: A Data-Driven Analysis of Range Anxiety and Consumer Purchase Decisions

## 📌 Project Overview

Electric vehicle (EV) adoption is growing globally, yet many consumers remain hesitant to purchase EVs due to concerns such as range anxiety, charging infrastructure, affordability, and charging convenience.

This project analyzes consumer survey data to identify the key factors influencing EV purchase decisions. By leveraging Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI, the project develops actionable marketing and business recommendations that can help EV manufacturers improve customer acquisition and accelerate EV adoption.

## 🎯 Business Objective

To identify the key drivers influencing electric vehicle purchase decisions and provide data-driven marketing strategies that improve EV adoption.
