# EV Consumer Analytics - Insights Register

## INS-001

### Business Question
Does environmental concern influence EV purchase intention?

### Key Finding
Consumers with higher environmental concern exhibit significantly higher EV purchase intention (41.4%) compared with consumers having the lowest environmental concern (2.1%).

### Business Impact
High

### Recommendation
Develop sustainability-focused marketing campaigns targeting environmentally conscious consumers.

---

## INS-002

### Business Question
Does range anxiety influence EV purchase intention?

### Key Finding
Consumers with low range anxiety are substantially more likely to purchase an EV than consumers with high range anxiety.

### Business Impact
High

### Recommendation
Educate customers on real-world EV range, charging infrastructure, and battery technology.

---

## INS-003

### Business Question
Does annual income influence EV purchase intention?

### Key Finding
Higher-income consumers show greater EV purchase intention than lower-income consumers.

### Business Impact
High

### Recommendation
Target premium and upper-middle-income consumers while offering financing options for lower-income customers.

---

## INS-004

### Business Question
Does home charging influence EV purchase intention?

### Key Finding
Consumers with home charging availability exhibit higher EV purchase intention.

### Business Impact
Medium

### Recommendation
Partner with home charger providers and promote home charging solutions.

---

## INS-005

### Business Question
Do government subsidies influence EV purchase intention?

### Key Finding
Consumers eligible for government subsidies show significantly higher EV purchase intention than those without subsidies.

### Business Impact
High

### Recommendation
Prominently communicate subsidy eligibility and estimated savings during the customer purchase journey.
